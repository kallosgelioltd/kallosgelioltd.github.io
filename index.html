Materials with presence and performance

Laterite-stabilised earth blocks bring thermal mass, strength and a natural texture that ages well. Timber provides warmth, lighter structure, shading, ceilings, doors, windows and shutters. Other local materials are chosen where they enhance both comfort and character. The result feels considered, not generic.

Design that invites you outside as well as in

Orientation, deep overhangs, ventilation, verandas, balconies and gardens are shaped so breeze moves, harsh sun is blocked, and shaded outdoor rooms become places you actually want to spend time. Comfort and delight sit together.

Lower cost without losing quality

Less heat buildup means cooling runs fewer hours. Well-protected walls and timber need less frequent repainting and repair. You keep the character and spend less maintaining it over the years.

Who We Are

Kallos Gelio Ltd

Kallos Gelio Ltd creates buildings that belong in Ghana — comfortable in the heat, satisfying to live with, and straightforward to maintain over time.

We design and build mainly with laterite-stabilised earth blocks, timber and other local materials, treating them as sources of both performance and character. Sustainable and climate-responsive choices shape the form, the textures and the way indoor and outdoor spaces work together.

Founded and led by Isaac Nuamah Darkwah, decisions on materials and site issues come directly from one accountable person. Milestone payments and regular updates keep you in clear control from the start.

The same approach works for schools, community buildings, and other projects where comfort in Ghana’s weather, lower long-term running and maintenance costs, and materials with character matter. We apply laterite-stabilised earth blocks, timber, and climate-responsive design wherever they improve how the building performs and feels.
